# Hi this is Udit Debangshi

PhD Scholar | Agricultural Water Management | Remote Sensing of Evapotranspiration | Precision Irrigation

My work focuses on the water use of crops and how to measure it at scales ranging from the field to the region.
My work combines eddy covariance towers, UAV thermal imagery, satellite data, and soil moisture
sensors to improve evapotranspiration (ET) models and irrigation decisions over the Ogallala Aquifer.
